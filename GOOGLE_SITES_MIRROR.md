# SSSWET — Google Sites mirror

The public Google Sites mirror is already published:

**https://sites.google.com/view/ssswet**

The canonical web application is also available at:

**https://gudjencio.github.io/SSSWET/**

The Google Sites mirror embeds the canonical application; it is not a separate research engine or database. The local Python service and its SQLite records remain on the machine where the service is running.
