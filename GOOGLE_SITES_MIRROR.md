# SSSWET — Google Sites mirror

The public Google Sites mirror is already published and available to visitors:

**http://sites.google.com/view/ssswet**

The canonical web application is also available at:

**https://gudjencio.github.io/SSSWET/**

The Google Sites mirror embeds the canonical application. Visitors do not need to create, configure or maintain a mirror. The mirror is a public access point, not a separate research engine or database. The local Python service and its SQLite research records remain on the machine where that service is running.
