# SSSWET technology catalogue

The public catalogue contains **100 research programmes** grouped into eight categories and browsable through translated category, subcategory and search controls.

## Categories and subcategories

| Category | Subcategories |
| --- | --- |
| Space science and exploration | Astrophysics and extreme gravity; propulsion and interstellar travel; space habitats and life support |
| Civilian protection and resilience | Nuclear-event protection; protective engineering |
| Energy systems | Clean energy generation; energy storage and conversion |
| Advanced materials | Adaptive and functional materials; structural and high-temperature materials |
| Life sciences and human systems | Regenerative medicine; human augmentation and assistive systems; animal health and welfare |
| Environmental stewardship | Ecosystem restoration; climate resilience and monitoring |
| Robotics and autonomous systems | Autonomous research and field robotics; assistive robotics and mobility |
| Knowledge and communication | Advanced scientific computing; communication and translation; public knowledge and health |

The category and subcategory labels are translated in the interface for English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Russian, Ukrainian, Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Hindi.

## Dedicated research areas

### Black holes and extreme astrophysics

The catalogue includes black-hole observation and physics, exploration-probe concepts, event-horizon imaging and theoretical study of craft resilience in extreme gravitational environments. These programmes must account for established general relativity, tidal forces, radiation, accretion environments, communication limits and the physical meaning of an event horizon. No vehicle capable of surviving passage through an event horizon is claimed to exist or to be physically achievable.

### Nuclear-event and radiation protection

The catalogue includes civilian blast resilience, radiation shielding, dosimetry networks, fallout detection and decontamination, electromagnetic-pulse resilience and extreme-heat protection. Research is limited to protective, emergency-response and public-safety applications. It does not include weapon design, construction or optimization.

## Research integrity

Catalogue entries are research themes, not validated products or promises of feasibility. The project distinguishes published evidence, hypotheses, simulations, prototypes and independent reviews. Missing project records do not prove that no external research exists. Every proposed test or prototype requires appropriate qualified oversight, safety assessment and legal review.

Funding contributions support research and do not constitute a product purchase or guarantee of delivery.
