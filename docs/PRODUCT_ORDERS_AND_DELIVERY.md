# Product orders, fair access and delivery

This policy applies only to a future technology that has been developed, reviewed and formally offered as a deliverable product. It does not apply to research contributions.

## 1. Separate research support from product orders

A contribution to research is voluntary support for investigation and development. It is not a deposit, reservation, pre-order or purchase contract. No delivery address is collected for a research contribution.

A product order may open only after the specific product has a documented specification, a defensible price, lawful supply and fulfilment arrangements, safety and regulatory review appropriate to its use, and published consumer terms.

## 2. Published price and payment choices

Every offer must state the base price, any applicable statutory taxes, delivery charges, payment options, estimated fulfilment period, warranty/support, limitations and cancellation/refund terms before the customer commits.

The intended payment choices are:

- **Instalments:** the customer pays the published base price in agreed parts. The total of the instalments must equal that base price; no instalment surcharge or hidden financing cost is added.
- **One-time payment:** the customer may be offered a separate, product-specific public-access contribution on top of the base price. Its purpose is to fund broader access and prevent release policy from being determined only by purchasing power.

The additional contribution is not a statutory tax. It must never be labelled or represented as a government tax unless legally confirmed by the competent authority. No rate is active by default. Before activation, the project must document the product-specific rationale, calculation, legal basis, consumer disclosures and payment-provider compatibility. The amount must be proportionate and visible before payment.

The one-time payment option is not a fast-track entitlement. A person paying immediately must not receive priority solely because they can pay faster. Allocation and release timing must follow the same published fair-access rules for all customers.

## 3. Fair allocation

Before accepting orders, publish an allocation policy suitable for the product. Possible mechanisms include a common order window, per-person purchase caps, randomized allocation among eligible orders, or staged release rounds. The selected method must be explained in advance and applied consistently.

A price supplement alone cannot ensure equal access. Allocation rules, quantity limits and transparent release windows are also required.

## 4. Payment reconciliation

Each payment is recorded against a private order reference. The order balance is calculated from confirmed, reconciled transactions only. A payment initiation, screenshot, email claim or browser redirect is not sufficient proof that funds have cleared.

The order may move to fully paid only after the payment provider's authoritative transaction record has been checked and the total received matches the accepted offer.

PayPal.Me is currently used for voluntary research contributions. It does not by itself provide SSSWET with automatic order-level payment reconciliation. A future product checkout must use a provider and integration capable of reliably identifying order references, amounts, currency, settlement status, refunds and chargebacks. Until that exists, product checkout remains disabled.

## 5. Delivery address and fulfilment

The delivery address is requested only when all of the following are true:

1. The product is genuinely available and the customer has accepted its final offer.
2. The complete agreed product price and any disclosed applicable charges have cleared.
3. The order has passed the project's fulfilment-readiness checks.

The address is private personal data. It must not be written to GitHub, public configuration, public research journals, public ledgers, analytics or issue trackers. It must be held only in an appropriately access-controlled fulfilment system, shared only with service providers who need it to deliver the order, and retained only for the period required by shipping, accounting, consumer and other applicable laws.

Providing an address is not proof of delivery. Delivery is closed only after appropriate carrier evidence and customer receipt confirmation (or another legally valid proof under the published terms) have been checked.

## 6. Gmail correspondence and deletion

The project contact address is ssswetindustries@gmail.com. Emails to institutions, scientific bodies or government authorities should be prepared only when their participation, authorization, advice or formal action is genuinely needed. Correspondence should include a useful project and technology description, current evidence and limitations, the reason for contact, proposed lawful next steps and the applicable safety/regulatory questions.

Drafts should be reviewed by an authorized person before sending. Gmail correspondence must not be automatically sent or permanently deleted by an unreviewed script.

A delivery conversation may be labelled as complete only after full payment has been reconciled and receipt of the technology has been verified. It may then be archived or moved to Trash under an authorized operator's review. Permanent deletion is not automatic: statutory retention, accounting, consumer claims, warranty, dispute and legal-hold duties may require retention. A script must never delete correspondence merely because payment is complete; receipt must also be verified.

## 7. Current activation status

No technology is currently offered for purchase through the public site. Product checkout, instalment collection, product-specific access contributions and delivery-address collection remain disabled until the product, pricing, legal review, payment integration, fair allocation and fulfilment arrangements are ready.

This policy is a project design statement, not legal advice. Applicable consumer, privacy, tax, product-safety, export-control and sector-specific rules must be reviewed for the actual product and destination country.
