# Research contributions

SSSWET welcomes voluntary support for its general research and development activities.

## Voluntary support

Contributions support the project as a whole. They are not investments, loans, product deposits, pre-orders or purchases of an existing technology. A contribution does not guarantee that a research objective will succeed or that a future product will be created.

The public website may direct supporters to the project's payment provider. The provider's checkout is authoritative for the amount, currency, fees, conversion, cancellation and payment confirmation. Do not send payment credentials or sensitive financial information through public issues, email forms or research channels.

## Future availability

No speculative research concept should be treated as a product offer. Any future offer would require a separate, specific description of the actual item, its verified capabilities and limitations, price, applicable terms, safety and legal review, and a lawful fulfilment process.

The project does not promise a product, delivery date, price, performance level or commercial availability for any research theme.

## Privacy

Public progress information should contain aggregate project-level information only. Donor names, email addresses, transaction references, account details and other personal payment data must not be published.

Personal information required for a lawful transaction must be handled separately through an appropriate access-controlled process and in accordance with applicable requirements.

## Research integrity

Public progress descriptions distinguish research activity from validated results. A concept, paper, simulation or prototype does not by itself establish that a technology works or is ready for use.

This page is a general project statement, not legal, tax, financial or consumer advice.
