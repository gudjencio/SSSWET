# Protective System Model Cards

**Status:** Conceptual research targets. None of these cards represents a tested or certified product.

## Aegis Modular

| Parameter | Standard | Reinforced | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Modular personal protection | Higher durability and environmental resilience | Specialist harsh-environment protection |
| Protection | Defined impact/environment hazards | Expanded tested hazard envelope | Narrow, explicitly tested hazard envelope |
| Mobility target | High | High with added mass documented | Acceptable only within defined task limits |
| Maintainability | Replaceable modules | Replaceable reinforced modules | Specialist serviceable modules |
| Evidence gate | Defined test plan | Independent comparative testing | Independent certification where applicable |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** more protective material can increase mass and thermal burden.

## Bastion Impact

| Parameter | Standard | Reinforced | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Impact and debris protection | Higher durability | Specialist environmental/impact configuration |
| Main evidence | Standardized impact tests | Comparative impact and environmental tests | Configuration-specific independent testing |
| Human factors | Fit and mobility | Fit, mobility and heat | Full task-duration assessment |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** greater energy absorption may increase thickness, mass or heat retention.

## Atlas Assist

| Parameter | Passive | Active | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assistance | Mechanical support | Powered assistance | Powered assistance plus validated sensing |
| Power | None | Battery system | Battery system with additional diagnostics |
| Safety | Mechanical fail-safe | Safe power-loss and disengagement | Redundant monitoring where justified |
| Validation | Ergonomic testing | Task-specific testing | Task-specific testing plus independent failure analysis |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** greater assistance increases system complexity, power requirements and potential failure modes.

## Lifeline Rescue

| Parameter | Compact | Heavy | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Mobility and light rescue equipment | Higher load assistance | Diagnostics and environmental hardening |
| Environment | Defined rescue environments | Defined demanding environments | Independently tested environments |
| Emergency behavior | Manual recovery | Manual recovery and emergency stop | Emergency stop plus diagnostics |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** higher load capacity must not compromise balance, evacuation speed or safe removal.

## Sentinel Environment

| Parameter | Climate | Containment | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary purpose | Weather and thermal protection | Defined contamination hazards | Specialist multi-hazard configuration |
| Certification | Applicable environmental tests | Hazard-specific certification | Each hazard independently validated |
| Mobility | High priority | Task-specific | Task-specific and duration-tested |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** stronger isolation can reduce breathability, dexterity and endurance.

## Guardian Monitor

| Parameter | Standard | Reinforced | Extreme |
|---|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Environmental and strain alerts | More robust sensing and logging | Specialist multi-sensor configuration |
| Diagnostic status | Non-diagnostic | Non-diagnostic | Non-diagnostic unless separately regulated and authorized |
| Evidence | Accuracy and usability tests | Comparative sensor validation | Configuration-specific validation |
| Privacy | Data minimization | Data minimization and access controls | Strongest justified controls |
| Price | Costed scenario | Costed scenario | Costed scenario |

**Key trade-off:** additional sensors can improve coverage while increasing false alarms, power use, maintenance and privacy exposure.

## Common evidence rule

No model receives a numerical protection rating, military designation or official price from this document alone. Every observed or verified value must be tied to a configuration, test method, date, source and reviewer.
