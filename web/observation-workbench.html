    2. Provenance and clock audit

    Capture sessions are isolated. Device wall-clock anchors from different sessions are not assumed comparable; the report never infers a global chronology.

    3. GPS quality diagnostics

    Flags identify records or segments for review. They do not delete observations or establish that a location is wrong.

    4. Temporal coverage and route metrics

    Sampling gaps describe recorded cadence only; they do not prove that no event occurred during an interval.

    5. Duplicate review candidates

    Exact and near-duplicate candidates are suggestions for human review. Nothing is merged, hidden or deleted.

    6. Privacy-adjusted export

    Choose what to include in a new copy. The imported source remains unchanged. The default profile reduces location and time precision and removes free-text descriptions and detailed sensor values.
